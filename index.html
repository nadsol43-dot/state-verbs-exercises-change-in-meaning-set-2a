Grammar guide & how to use

Type only the missing words, including a subject when the bracketed prompt includes it. Contractions and full forms are accepted. Press Enter in a text field to check that sentence.

States usually use simple forms: know, belong, want, believe, understand. Actions can use continuous forms when they are in progress.

Meaning changes the grammar: “I think” = my opinion; “I’m thinking” = considering. “I have” = own; “I’m having” = experiencing or doing. “It tastes good” = a quality; “I’m tasting it” = an action. “You’re being…” describes current behaviour.
